Our Journey

A Legacy of Innovation

92

1992

The Foundation

Founded in a small precision grinding shop, Kawa Tooling Systems began with a singular focus: manufacturing solid carbide end mills that could outlast the competition in aggressive steel applications.

08

2008

The AlTiN Breakthrough

Kawa establishes its first in-house PVD coating facility, developing proprietary Aluminum Titanium Nitride nano-coatings that revolutionized tool life for our aerospace clients machining Inconel.

18

2018

Global Scale

Expansion reaches critical mass with the opening of our 4th global manufacturing hub, granting us a completely localized supply chain for our North American and European OEM automotive partners.

Now

Today

Autonomous Precision

Integrating robotics and AI into our inspection workflows. Every tool leaving our facility is laser-scanned, digitally verified, and tied to a unique lifecycle data profile.
