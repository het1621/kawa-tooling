What is Precision Tooling?

Precision tooling refers to the specialized manufacturing of custom instruments, molds, dies, and hardware required to create large-scale industrial components with extremely tight tolerances. At Kawa Tooling Systems, we define this as the marriage of mathematical accuracy and material science.

In modern manufacturing, where a variation of a single micron can mean the difference between a successful flight and a system failure, precision tooling serves as the bedrock of reliability. Our systems are engineered to withstand millions of cycles while maintaining the exact geometric integrity required by world-class aerospace and medical standards.
